# Camera Stream Phrasing

Use these terms when translating Chinese technical messages about camera/video or retail AI systems.

## Common Terms

- `鱼眼`: `fisheye camera` or `fisheye view`
- `去畸变`: `dewarp` / `dewarping`
- `四宫格`: `quad view` or `four-way split view`
- `全景`: `panorama` / `panoramic view`
- `码流`: `stream` or `video stream`
- `拉流`: `pull the stream` or `access the stream`
- `推流`: `push the stream`
- `截图/样张`: `sample image`, `snapshot`, or `frame capture`
- `反编译码流`: usually `reverse-engineer/decode the camera stream`; reserve `decompile` for binaries, firmware, APK/JAR/ELF, or executables.

## Coordination Phrases

- `上去研究研究`: `hop on and poke around` for informal engineer chat; `access it to investigate` for formal contexts.
- `投屏/共享屏幕`: `share their screen` / `screenshare`, not `cast` unless literally casting to a TV.
- `后台`: `backend`, `admin portal`, or `management console` depending on context.
- `兜底方案`: `fallback plan`, `fallback solution`, `last resort`, or `absolute fallback` depending on tone.

## Pitfalls

- Use `Hanwha` for the video security vendor, not `Hanhua`, unless the source explicitly refers to a different entity.
- Avoid translating `dewarp` as generic `correct distortion` when the user is discussing the product feature name.
- Prefer short copy-pasteable phrasing for work chat; add terminology notes only when ambiguity would change the technical meaning.
